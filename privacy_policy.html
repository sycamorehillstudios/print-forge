Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 (version 1.0.7).

Print Forge ("the app") is published by Sycamore Hill Studios. This policy explains what information the app and the Google services it uses collect, how that information is used, the choices you have, and how to get in touch.

Summary

  • Your game progress stays on your device. We do not run servers and we do not receive your gameplay data, crash logs or any personal information.
  • The only ads are optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads.
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and can change that choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing; we never see your payment details.

What the app stores on your device

All gameplay progress (currencies, printers, gallery, wall of shame, research, prestige, purchased upgrades, VIP status and settings) is saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else; the app has no cloud save. Uninstalling the app removes this data, and Settings → Game → Reset save clears your progress.

Diagnostics log (stays on your device)

To help us fix problems, the app keeps a diagnostic log on your device. It records gameplay events (for example screens opened, prints started and sold, rewards claimed, purchase buttons tapped, whether ads may be shown after the consent step) and the text of any errors the app encounters, together with a session number and timestamps. It does not record your name, email address, advertising ID or location.

  • The log never leaves your device: the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service and does not send this log anywhere.
  • It is stored in the app's private storage, capped in size (older entries are dropped automatically) and deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • You can view it, copy it to your clipboard, or clear it at any time under Settings → Diagnostics. Copying only places the text on your clipboard; it is shared only if you choose to paste it somewhere, for example in an email to us.

Ads (Google AdMob, rewarded only)

Print Forge shows rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one (for example "Watch & earn" in the Shop or "Watch ad for 2×" on the welcome-back screen). Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. The app does not show banner, interstitial or app-open ads.

When the Google Mobile Ads SDK is active, Google may collect and process the following data from your device:

  • Advertising ID: your device's resettable advertising identifier (Android advertising ID).
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address. The app does not request or use your device's precise (GPS) location.
  • Device and app information and diagnostics: such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and performance and crash data related to the ads SDK.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, watched to completion or tapped.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, to measure ads and provide analytics on ad performance, and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse. Where you have given consent (or where consent is not required), Google may also use it to personalise ads. This data is collected by Google, not by Sycamore Hill Studios; the app does not receive your advertising ID or location. Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent for players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform. Each time the app starts it checks with Google whether consent is needed where you are. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can accept, reject or choose which purposes and partners to allow. The ads SDK is not started, and no ad is requested, until this step allows it.

You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options (this entry appears where Google's consent rules apply). Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy gems, the Starter Pack or the VIP Pass, the transaction is handled by Google Play Billing. Print Forge never sees or stores your payment method, name or billing address. Google Play gives the app a purchase record (the product bought, an order ID and a purchase token), which the app uses on your device to grant and, when you tap Restore purchases, to re-apply what you bought. Google's processing of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms of service. The VIP Pass lets you claim rewarded-ad bonuses without watching a video.

Fonts (Google Fonts)

The app's typefaces (Fraunces, Inter and JetBrains Mono) are downloaded from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) the first time they are needed and then cached on your device. These downloads send Google your IP address and standard request information (such as the app's user agent); no gameplay or account data is included. See developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy.

Ratings prompt

The app may occasionally ask Google Play to show its in-app review prompt. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play under your Google account; the app is not told whether or what you rated.

Notifications

The app can send you local reminders, for example when your printers have finished a batch or a daily reward is ready. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; no notification content is sent to us or to any third party. Android asks your permission before the first reminder, and you can turn them off at any time in your system notification settings for Print Forge.

Children

Print Forge is intended for a general audience. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (EEA, UK and Switzerland), or your device's ad settings elsewhere.
  • Don't watch ads: rewarded ads are always optional; the game is fully playable without them.
  • Diagnostics log: view or clear it under Settings → Diagnostics.
  • Reset save: clears your game progress under Settings → Game → Reset save.
  • Uninstall: removes the app, your save and the diagnostics log.
  • Manage purchases: purchases and refunds are managed through the Google Play account that made the purchase.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. Substantive changes will be announced in a future update's release notes.
