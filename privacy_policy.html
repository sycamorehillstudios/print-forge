Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026.

Print Forge ("the app") is published by Sycamore Hill Studios. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can get in touch.

What the app stores on your device

All gameplay progress — currencies, gallery, wall of shame, research, prestige, purchased upgrades, VIP status — is saved locally on your device in the app's sandboxed documents directory. It is not transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly opt in to a cloud-save feature (not enabled in this build). Uninstalling the app removes this data.

In-app purchases

If you purchase gems, a VIP pass, or other items, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Print Forge does not see or store your payment method. Google Play provides the app with a receipt confirming the purchase; the app uses that receipt to grant the items you bought.

Ads

Print Forge displays ads served by Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of the in-game Shop tab.
  • Interstitial ads shown occasionally between prints (rate-limited to at most one every six minutes, never during the onboarding window).
  • Rewarded ads that you can opt into voluntarily from the Shop tab to earn a small in-game currency reward.

AdMob may collect a limited set of identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on IP, and basic device information) to deliver and measure ads. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and the AdMob/AdSense partner addendum at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or limit your advertising ID through your device's system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics and crash reports

Print Forge itself does not send analytics or crash reports to our servers. The Google Play services and AdMob SDK that the app depends on may transmit a limited amount of diagnostic data to Google as described above. If a future release integrates additional analytics (for example Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics), this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how you can opt out.

Children

Print Forge is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and it will be deleted.

Your choices

  • Reset save — clears all local gameplay data. Available under Settings → Game → Reset save.
  • Uninstall — removes the app and every local save.
  • Manage purchases — purchases, refunds, and subscription cancellations are managed through the Google Play Store account that made the purchase.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. Substantive changes will be announced in a future update's release notes.
